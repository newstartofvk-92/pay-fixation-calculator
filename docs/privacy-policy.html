Privacy Policy

NiyamMitra – Pay Fixation Calculator

Effective date: 9 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NiyamMitra – Pay Fixation Calculator (“the App”) handles information when you use the App.

1. Information stored by the App

The App does not require an account, registration, or login. Calculation history that you choose to save is stored locally on your device. This may include the name you enter, employee category, pay level, basic pay, promotion date, DNI date, and calculated results.

The App does not operate its own server or database for this calculation history. You can delete individual saved calculations or clear the saved history from within the App.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to users who have not purchased the lifetime ad-free option. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interactions, and other information needed to provide, measure, and protect advertising services.

Advertising-related data handling is governed in part by Google’s applicable policies and privacy controls. The App does not sell your personal information to advertisers.

3. In-app purchase

The App offers an optional one-time purchase to remove advertisements. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Payment card, billing, and other payment details are handled by Google Play; the App does not receive your payment card information.

4. Third-party services

The App uses third-party services from Google for advertising and Google Play Billing. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data security and retention

Locally saved calculation history remains on your device until you delete it or the App’s local data is removed. Because the App does not maintain an online account or server-side calculation-history database, the developer does not retain that locally saved history on its own servers.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-purpose utility intended for adults and professionals. It is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App’s features, services, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date.

8. Contact

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or the App’s privacy practices, please use the developer contact information provided on the App’s Google Play Store listing.
